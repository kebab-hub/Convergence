# Optional Integrations

All integrations are optional unless explicitly promoted to a hard dependency in a future design decision.

## Create
Role: early mechanical automation and kinetic infrastructure.

Planned concepts:
- mechanical variants of selected Convergence machines;
- SU ↔ electrical conversion through motors/dynamos;
- sequenced assembly recipes;
- belts/depots/basins as alternate process routes.

## Applied Energistics 2
Role: logistics, autocrafting, computation and late-game information infrastructure.

Planned concepts:
- machine/multiblock interfaces;
- chemical/fluid/Aether interfaces where appropriate;
- specialized storage cells;
- computation/data integration;
- spatial technology integration.

## IC2 Classic
Role: electrical progression, power infrastructure and optional nuclear/electrical interoperability.

Planned concepts:
- compatible electrical machines where technically sensible;
- power conversion boundaries;
- cross-recipes and shared material tags;
- reactor-related integration where balance permits.

## JEI
Role: recipe discoverability.
Every custom processing system should have readable JEI support before it becomes a major progression gate.

## Botania / Ars Nouveau
Potential later bridges from Mana or Source into Aether-related systems.
These must remain optional and must not trivialize Convergence's own Aether progression.
